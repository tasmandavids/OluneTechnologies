# Olune — Pre-Launch Campaign Strategy

**Written:** 1 September 2026
**Runway:** 14 weeks · Week 1 begins 1 Sept · Launch week is 1–7 December
**Channels:** Instagram (awareness) · Email (conversion)
**Starting position:** zero followers, zero list

---

## 1. The strategic problem, stated honestly

Three things make this campaign harder than a normal SaaS launch, and the plan has to be built around them rather than pretending they aren't there.

**We are starting from nothing, into a small market.** New Zealand has somewhere in the low hundreds of dance studios large enough to pay for software. That is not a volume game. Every one of them is reachable by name — which means the campaign is less "build an audience and hope" and more "become known to a list of specific people, one at a time." Instagram's job is to make us *recognisable* when the email arrives. Email's job is to close.

**December is the worst month to ask a studio owner to buy anything.** NZ studios run Term 4 from October to mid-December, and November–December is recital and concert season — the single most stretched period in their year. A studio owner in late November is not evaluating software. She is chasing costume sizes and selling tickets.

This is the most important planning fact in the document, and it changes the shape of the campaign:

> **December is the launch. January–February is the conversion window.** Term 1 enrolment, when studios rebuild their rolls and their admin pain is freshest, is when studios actually switch systems.

So the December launch is a *credibility* event, not a revenue event. We use the free-until-general-release period to get studios genuinely running on Olune through recital season, so that in January they aren't evaluating a product — they're already using one. The recital-season pain we're launching into is also the pain Olune's events, costumes and production modules exist to solve. That's not bad timing; it's the demo writing itself.

**We have no customers yet, so we have no proof.** Everything in Phase 1 exists to manufacture legitimate proof: real studios, real screens, real quotes. Nothing in this plan invents a testimonial or a number.

## 2. What we are actually selling

Not "studio management software." Every competitor says that.

**Positioning statement**

> Olune is studio software built by someone who has run the studio office. It's calm where the others are cluttered, it's priced in New Zealand dollars, and it never charges you more for having more students.

**The four wedges** — in priority order. Every piece of content should be traceable to one of these.

| # | Wedge | The line | Why it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Unlimited students, always** | "We don't charge you for growing." | Jackrabbit prices by student count. A studio that has a great enrolment term gets a bigger bill. Olune's tiers gate capability, never headcount. This is a structural difference they can't match without rebuilding their business model. |
| 2 | **Built by a studio person** | "Written by someone who has done a Sunday-night roll reconciliation." | Tasman trained at the Vaganova Academy, danced professionally, and co-founded a ballet school. No competitor's founder has stood in the room. This is the most defensible asset we have and it costs nothing to deploy — **but every line of it needs verifying before it goes on camera. See §10.5.** |
| 3 | **Priced here, in your money** | "NZ$29 a month. GST included. No exchange rate." | Incumbents bill in USD. Studio owners feel this every month and hate it. |
| 4 | **Calm by design** | "Quiet is a feature." | The category is a wall of tabs and toolbars. Olune's restraint is a real product decision and it photographs beautifully — which matters enormously on Instagram. |

**What we do not say.** Nothing about AI unless we're showing it working. No feature-count comparisons ("200+ features") — that's their game and it contradicts wedge 4. No urgency theatre, no exclamation marks, no countdown-timer panic. The brand guide is explicit: *confident, not loud.*

**And nothing that isn't built.** See `05-claims-check.md`. The first draft of this campaign promised offline rolls, automatic absence notifications, self-serve data export and a competitions module — none of which exist. That check runs again before every phase goes live.

## 3. Audience

**Primary — NZ dance studio owner-operators.** Woman, 30s–50s, typically a former dancer, runs 80–400 students, teaches as well as administrates, does the books at 9pm. Currently on a mix of Jackrabbit/Studio Pro, a spreadsheet, a WhatsApp group, and memory. She is on Instagram every day because her studio's account lives there.

Her real problem is not "I lack software." It is *"I am doing four hours of admin a week that I resent, and I'm scared of moving because the migration will land on me."* Migration fear is the number-one objection this campaign must dismantle.

**Secondary — Australian studios.** Same profile, ten times the market, same pain, and they read the same Instagram accounts. We build the AU list from day one but we do not dilute the NZ-first messaging to get them. AU-specific pricing and messaging is a post-launch workstream.

**Tertiary — adjacent NZ studio types.** Yoga, pilates, martial arts, gymnastics, swim schools. They find us; we don't chase them yet. Every one that joins the waitlist is evidence for the vertical-expansion story later.

**Explicitly not the audience right now:** parents, students, or dancers. They are the *content subject* sometimes, never the buyer. Vanity followers from dancers will make the account look successful and convert nothing. Watch this — it's the most likely way this campaign quietly fails.

## 4. The funnel

```
INSTAGRAM                 →   WAITLIST              →   EMAIL NURTURE        →   FOUNDING STUDIO       →   PAID
Reels, carousels,             Landing page,             Weekly "Front Desk"      Onboarded, data           General
founder POV, studio           one field: email,         + automated welcome      imported, running         release,
proof                         plus optional             sequence                 live and free             Dec/Jan
                              studio name
        ↑                            ↑                                                   ↓
   Cold email to             Lead magnet:                                        Their screens and words
   named NZ studios          "The Studio Term                                    become Instagram content
   (Sequence B)              Reset" pack                                         (with permission)
```

Two things to notice about this diagram.

**The loop at the bottom is the engine.** Founding studios don't just convert — they produce the content that recruits the next ones. This is why Phase 1's real target is founding studios, not followers.

**Cold email feeds the top, not the bottom.** We are not cold-emailing a sales pitch. We are inviting named studios into a founding cohort with a free product and the founder's phone number. That is a defensible, welcome email; a discount-code blast is not.

## 5. Phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4 · 1–28 September)
*Nobody is watching yet. Use that.*

- Instagram account live, bio, highlights, first 12 posts. Post daily to build a back catalogue so the first real visitor finds a full grid, not three posts.
- Founder story published — the strongest single piece of content we own, once verified.
- Waitlist landing page live with one field.
- Cold outreach Sequence B to the first 50 named NZ studios, sent by hand, personalised.
- **Target: 12 founding studios agreed. 250 followers. 80 emails.**

Followers are the *least* important number in this phase. Twelve studios who reply to Tasman is a better outcome than a thousand followers.

### Phase 2 — Proof (Weeks 5–9 · 29 September – 2 November)
*Turn founding studios into evidence.*

- Founding studios onboarded and running. Each one produces a screen, a quote, or a number.
- Lead magnet ships: **"The Studio Term Reset"** — a practical pack (term-fee calculator, enrolment email templates, a roll-reconciliation checklist) that is useful whether or not you ever buy Olune. This is the list engine.
- Weekly newsletter **The Front Desk** begins.
- Cold outreach extends to studios 51–150.
- First Reels pushing on wedge 1 (unlimited students) — the most shareable message we have.
- **Target: 30 founding studios. 700 followers. 250 emails.**

### Phase 3 — Countdown (Weeks 10–13 · 3–30 November)
*Recital season. Be useful, not loud.*

- Content pivots hard into recital and concert season: ticketing, costume tracking, running a door, production admin. Maximum usefulness at maximum stress = maximum saves and shares.
- Feature reveals, one per week, each tied to a wedge and each checked against what has actually shipped.
- Pricing published and explained openly (a post about *why* we don't charge per student outperforms any feature post).
- Launch week teased from Week 12, not Week 10. Four weeks of countdown is a fortnight too long.
- **Target: 45 founding studios. 1,200 followers. 450 emails.**

### Week 14 — Launch (1–7 December)
- Monday: general release announcement, IG + email + the founder's personal networks.
- Tuesday–Thursday: one wedge per day, Reels + email.
- Friday: founding studios named and thanked publicly. This is the proof post.
- Sunday: the quiet one — a note about what comes next, and the January Term 1 offer opening.

**Target: 60+ studios on the platform. 1,500 followers. 600 emails. 15+ converting to paid by end of February.**

## 6. Content pillars (Instagram)

Five pillars, in rotation. Every post belongs to exactly one.

| Pillar | What it is | Share of posts | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Studio Office** | Practical admin help with no product in it. Fee structures, chasing late payments, enrolment scripts, term planning. | 30% | Saves and shares. This is what gets us reached. |
| **Built in the Open** | The build log. Screens, decisions, why-we-chose-this — including what isn't finished. | 25% | Trust and differentiation. |
| **The Calm Studio** | Brand and philosophy. Quiet, the moon, restraint, the case against bloat. | 20% | Recognition and taste. Makes the account look like something. |
| **Studio Voices** | Founding studios — their rooms, their words, their numbers. | 15% | Proof. Scales up from Phase 2. |
| **Ask Olune** | Q&A from DMs and comments. Migration fear, "can I move mid-term," pricing. | 10% | Objection handling in public. |

**Cadence: 5 posts/week.** Three Reels, one carousel, one single image or quote card. Stories daily — behind the screen, polls, reposting studio content. One collaborative post per month with a founding studio once Phase 2 starts.

Reels carry reach; carousels carry saves; stories carry relationship. The grid is the shopfront and must look like the brand guide — Midnight, Iris, Halo Cream, Fraunces headlines, plenty of empty space.

## 7. Email programme

| Sequence | Trigger | Length | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A · Waitlist welcome** | Joins waitlist | 5 emails / 12 days | Turn a curious signup into someone who wants a founding place |
| **B · Cold outreach** | Manual, named studios | 4 touches / 3 weeks | Invite specific NZ studios into the founding cohort |
| **C · Founding onboarding** | Accepts a founding place | 3 emails / 10 days | Get them actually live — an unused account is worse than no account |
| **D · Launch week** | Whole list | 4 emails / 7 days | Convert the nurtured list at general release |
| **The Front Desk** | Weekly, from Week 5 | Ongoing | Stay useful between campaigns; the list's reason to stay subscribed |

Copy for all of these is in `04-email-sequences.md`.

**Sending rules.** Plain-text-feeling HTML, one CTA per email, from a person (`tasman@olune.co.nz` — the production domain, *not* `olune.app`) rather than a brand. Cold outreach goes out in batches of 20 by hand in Phase 1 — deliverability matters more than volume when the list is 150 names, and a personalised first line is what gets a reply. Set up SPF/DKIM/DMARC before the first send.

## 8. Metrics

Tracked weekly. Only the first three actually matter.

**Primary**

- Founding studios *live and using the product* (not signed up — using)
- Email subscribers
- Reply rate on cold outreach

**Secondary**

- Instagram followers, saves, shares (saves > likes, always)
- Waitlist conversion rate from profile visits
- Newsletter open and click rate

**Vanity — report but never optimise for**

- Likes, impressions, follower count in isolation

The one number to watch for trouble: **the ratio of studio owners to dancers in the follower base.** If the account starts growing fast, check who's arriving before celebrating.

## 9. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| Recital season makes studios unreachable in Nov | High | Assume it. Front-load acquisition into Sept–Oct; make Nov content useful rather than salesy; hold the real push for January. |
| Audience fills with dancers, not owners | High | Content aimed squarely at the office, not the studio floor. Check the ratio monthly. |
| Migration fear blocks conversion | High | A migration guarantee: we do the import for founding studios, by hand, free. Say it in every sequence. |
| **No Terms of Service, DPA or refund policy** | Certain — true today | **Launch blocker, not a copy problem.** Sequence D converts strangers to paid, and Olune is a processor of children's data for every studio. `MARKET_READINESS.md` §2.1 has said this since 6 August. Must exist before 1 December. |
| **"Free until general release" isn't what the code does** | Certain | New studios get a 14-day trial, then lock out (`0119_studio_plans.sql`). Sixty September signups become sixty lockouts on day fifteen unless someone comps each one by hand. Either auto-comp until GA, or change the promise. **Fix before Sequence B batch 2.** |
| **Selling recital-night reliability with no monitoring** | High | No error monitoring, no uptime checks, no status page (`MARKET_READINESS.md` §1.1, §1.4). Weeks 9–10 content promises dependability at exactly the moment failure is most visible. Ship Sentry and a status page before the concert-season posts run. |
| Marketing claims outrun the product | High — it already happened | `05-claims-check.md` is the standing list. No campaign claim goes live for a feature that isn't shipped, and the check re-runs before each phase. |
| Founder capacity — one person doing product and marketing | High | Batch content monthly. Phase 1 exists partly to build a back catalogue for when November gets busy. |

Confirm every line before it goes on camera; drop "first New Zealander" unless it's documented; and settle whether the school is named (Nova Dance Academy) or unnamed ("a ballet school in Christchurch") — the drafts currently do both. The public `/team` page names no founder at all and will contradict the launch Reel.
