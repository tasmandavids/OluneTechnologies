## After launch — the real conversion window

The calendar above ends at launch. **It is not the end of the campaign.** NZ studios switch systems in January and February, when Term 1 enrolment forces them to touch every record they own. Budget for a second campaign covering **11 January – 28 February 2027**, aimed squarely at that window, with:

- A "Term 1 Reset" offer for studios who onboard before the first day of term
- Migration-week content — the fear-dismantling series, run properly
- Founding-studio case studies with a full term of real numbers behind them
- The first paid-acquisition test (Meta ads to a lookalike of the founding cohort)

Everything built between September and December is the raw material for that campaign. Plan it in late November while the December content is already batched and scheduled.

## Production notes

**Batch monthly.** Film four weeks of Reels in one afternoon. November will be busy for everyone including us — Weeks 10–14 should be shot and written by the end of October.

**Assets needed per week:** 3 Reels (30–60s), 1 carousel (5–8 slides), 1 still. Carousel slides use Fraunces for headlines, Hanken Grotesk for body, Halo Cream or Midnight grounds, Iris for the one accent.

**Reels rules:** hook in the first 1.5 seconds, on-screen text always (most viewing is silent), 30–45s is the sweet spot for this audience, native captions on. No trending audio that fights the brand — use quiet beds or the founder's voice.

**Hashtags.** Two sets, rotated, 8–12 per post, in the first comment:

`#dancestudioowner #studioowner #dancestudionz #nzdance #balletschool #dancestudiolife #studiomanagement #dancestudioadmin #dancebusiness #studiosoftware`

`#dancestudioaustralia #dancestudiobusiness #danceteacherlife #studioowners #dancestudiomarketing #dancestudiotips #balletstudio #performingartsschool`
