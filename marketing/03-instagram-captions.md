**Slide copy**

### Tue 29 Sept · Carousel (8 slides) · Roll reconciliation in 15 minutes

**Slide copy**

1. *The Sunday-night job nobody talks about.*
2. You have a class list. You have who turned up. You have who's paid. Three lists that should agree and never quite do.
3. **Step 1.** Start from the enrolment list, never from memory or the roll book.
4. **Step 2.** Mark the three categories separately: absent-notified, absent-unexplained, and not-enrolled-but-attended. The third one is where money leaks.
5. **Step 3.** Anyone attending who isn't enrolled gets enrolled or invoiced this week. Not next week.
6. **Step 4.** Absent-unexplained, twice in a row, gets a message. That's your churn early-warning system.
7. **Step 5.** Reconcile against payments last, once the roll is true. Doing it first is why it takes two hours.
8. Fifteen minutes if you go in that order. Two hours if you don't.

**Caption**
```
Three lists that should agree and never quite do: who's enrolled, who turned up, who's paid.

The order you check them in is the whole difference between fifteen minutes and two hours. Enrolment list first, attendance second, payments last — because reconciling money against a roll you don't trust yet is how the evening disappears.

The category people skip is "attended but not enrolled." That's where the money leaks, and it's almost always the same three families.

Two unexplained absences in a row is your churn warning. Message them.
```
