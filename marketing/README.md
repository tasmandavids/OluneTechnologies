## Start here

1. Read `05-claims-check.md` §3 — there are four ops blockers that gate parts of this campaign, and one of them (the "free until December" promise) needs fixing before the second batch of cold emails goes out.
2. Answer the six decisions at the end of `01-campaign-strategy.md`.
3. Week 1 is account setup only. Nothing sends. That's the week to fix the blockers.

## The one strategic point worth arguing about

December is the launch, but **January and February are the conversion window.** NZ studios switch systems at Term 1 enrolment, not in the middle of recital season. This campaign is built to arrive in January with studios already using the product rather than evaluating it — and there's a second campaign to plan for 11 January – 28 February.
